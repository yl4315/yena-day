개인정보처리방침 (Privacy Policy) · 문의

Yena's Day is a private Android calendar and daily-records app used by a family: a block calendar synced with Google Calendar, checklists, anniversaries, habit and health records kept only on the phone, voice input and home-screen widgets. It is not published on an app store and has no server of its own. See the Privacy Policy.
